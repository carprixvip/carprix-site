Guardian Monitoring

Optional, at the member's request: your co-responsible party can follow the vehicle's location and driving data through Carprix — available after delivery. Request it here if you did not when you applied.

Before you request it.
  • Guardian Monitoring is informational: the co-responsible party sees the vehicle's location, speed, trips and event alerts. Nothing acts on the vehicle, and it changes no responsibility of either of you under the agreement.
  • Carprix confirms the election by e-mail to both of you; it then forms part of your Subscription Agreement. Withdrawing or narrowing it later requires notice from both the member and the co-responsible party.
  • Available where the vehicle is equipped for it. If your Subscription Agreement states a Guardian Monitoring fee, it applies from activation; no fee applies where the member is under 25. Details: Subscription Agreement, Section 19.7, and the Privacy Policy.

Stop renewal or end early

Give notice of non-renewal at any time, online, right here — or request an early end to your membership.

How it works.
  • Your membership has a minimum commitment of 3 quarterly payments — 9 months, counted from the delivery of your vehicle.
  • Non-renewal. Once the 3 payments are made, your notice takes effect at the end of the quarter already paid (received at least 7 days before that date); the membership does not renew after it. Before the 3 payments, a notice of non-renewal takes effect at the end of the minimum term.
  • Ending early. Before the 3 payments, the membership can end only once the remaining balance of the minimum commitment — the quarterly payments still due — is settled. We issue an invoice with a secure payment link, due in 7 days; once it is paid, the vehicle is returned intact at the meeting point confirmed at signing and the membership ends on return.
  • Your membership stays active until the effective date. Quarterly fees are not refunded after delivery.
  • We confirm the next steps on screen and by e-mail. Full details in the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

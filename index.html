Co-responsible party

Two signatures. One agreement.

Every membership is signed by the member and a co-responsible party (co-signer) — often a parent or another family member. Together they are jointly and severally responsible for the vehicle's condition, for lawful driving and for every fee, fine and consequence of its use — wherever it is driven and whoever drives it. Both sign electronically, in sequence: the member first, then the co-responsible party.

Optional · at the member's request

Guardian Monitoring

At the member's request, the co-responsible party can follow the vehicle's location and driving data through Carprix — available after delivery. Request it when you apply, or at any point during the membership.

Drivers under 25: the insurance deductible may be up to USD 5,000 per claim, a refundable Additional Security Deposit equal to that deductible is paid before delivery, and Guardian Monitoring is included at no fee for members under 25 — see the Refund & Cancellation Policy, Section 9.

What you'll need

  • A co-responsible party (co-signer) — an adult who signs the agreement with you and shares full responsibility for it.
  • Government-issued ID for both signers; identity is verified electronically when you sign in DocuSign.
  • Drivers aged 21 or over, holding a licence valid where the vehicle is registered for at least two years.
  • A card or US bank account for the first quarter, paid in USD through Stripe Checkout before the agreement is sent.
  • Proof of insurance meeting the Insurance & Driver Requirements, delivered before the vehicle is released.

Insurance is arranged and paid for by the member; Carprix provides none. Permitted use and driver requirements: see the Subscription Agreement.

FAQ

Good questions.

What is the minimum term?

Three quarterly payments — nine months, counted from the delivery of your vehicle. The first quarter is paid at checkout; the second and third are charged three and six months after delivery. After that the membership renews quarter by quarter until you give notice of non-renewal.

What happens after I pay?

You receive the Subscription Agreement by e-mail from DocuSign — you sign first, then your co-responsible party. We then complete the compliance review: identity, driving licence and proof of insurance. Carprix may decline an application after review; in that case the payment is refunded in full.

Where is the vehicle delivered, and where can I drive it?

Vehicles are sourced globally from our owner network; availability and demand decide where a vehicle comes from and where you drive it. When you sign the agreement you are informed of the handover meeting point, and the same applies to the return. The vehicle is handed over within 30 days of acceptance and stays within the country where it is registered.

Who owns the vehicles?

Carprix is a platform. Vehicles are supplied by owners in our global network under separate agreements with Carprix; your contract is with Carprix, LLC only, and you deal with Carprix only — one agreement, one point of contact.

Can I end the membership early?

You may stop renewal at any time from Manage billing; it takes effect at the end of the minimum term or of the quarter already paid, whichever is later. Ending the membership before the three payments is possible only by settling the remaining balance of the three quarters and returning the vehicle intact.

Quarterly fees are not refunded after delivery. Full details in the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Does the membership renew automatically?

Yes. After the minimum term, your card or bank account is charged at the start of each quarter and the membership renews for a further quarter unless you have given notice of non-renewal. You receive a reminder e-mail at least seven days before each renewal charge.

Who is responsible for insurance, fines and the vehicle's condition?

The member and the co-responsible party, jointly and severally. Insurance is entirely the member's responsibility and cost; proof of cover is required before the vehicle is released. Fines, tolls, damage beyond fair wear and tear and every other consequence of use are for the member and the co-responsible party. Details in the Subscription Agreement.

Is Carprix suited to students abroad?

Yes. A member studying away from home drives; a parent — or another eligible adult — signs as co-responsible party, electronically and from anywhere, and both are jointly and severally responsible under one agreement. Driver requirements (age, licence history, insurance) apply to the member as to every driver; see the Subscription Agreement.

At the member's request, Guardian Monitoring lets the co-responsible party follow the vehicle's location and driving data through Carprix — optional, available after delivery, and it can be requested at any point during the membership.

Does Carprix track the vehicle?

Vehicles may be fitted with GPS and telematics equipment. Where fitted, location, mileage, speed and driving-event data are collected for asset protection and compliance — to protect the vehicle, administer the agreement and respond to accidents or theft. Access for the co-responsible party is offered as Guardian Monitoring, at the member's request. What is collected, why and for how long is described in the Privacy Policy.

How can I pay?

By card or US bank account (ACH) through Stripe Checkout, in USD. The first quarter is paid before the agreement is sent for signature; the following quarters are charged automatically to the same payment method. Bank debits can take up to four business days to clear.
